FleetBus: a car's network and its cloud, at bench scale

Two STM32 "ECUs" run FreeRTOS on a real CAN bus. A third node rides in a 2025 Nissan Sentra and reads the car without ever writing to it. All three report to a fleet backend, which pushes signed firmware back down one node at a time, and the bootloader rolls back any version that fails its health check.

telemetry, up to the cloud firmware updates, down to your nodes read-only (the car itself)

Rules that don't bend

Never write to the car.Standard diagnostic requests only. No flashing, fuzzing or injected frames.
Update your nodes, never the car's.Your node in the Sentra can take updates. Nissan's modules are never written to, and fault injection stays on the bench.
Don't drain the battery.The OBD-II port is powered with the car off. Sleep or unplug the node.
Glanceable screens only.A passenger operates or films. Nothing mounted on the windshield.
